# Custom instruments — build budget

The act's custom-instrument program. Long-horizon by design: instruments are
part of the band from the start, realized one build at a time. Each
instrument gets its own sheet in this directory (copy TEMPLATE.md).

Design brief (stage-vision.md, "The role of live instrumentation"):
instruments are communication devices — visual/tactile anchors for sounds
in the track, and transient/attack reinforcement. Judge every build on
legibility and attack, not acoustic power; the wireless rule: playable in
motion; and the carry-on rule: breaks down to fit carry-on luggage
(drum heads max 13").

Builds serve the stage vision ([docs/stage-vision.md](../../docs/stage-vision.md));
phases are funded by reinvested act revenue per D13.

## Rules

- **Roster before dollars** — an instrument enters the table with a name and
  a concept line; costs attach only once it has a sheet with a real BOM.
- **Phase by stage debut** — money is planned per phase (what plays at the
  next show), never as one lump total.
- **Contingency** — every custom build carries +25% on its BOM estimate.
- **Tools amortize** — shared tools live in their own table below, not in any
  instrument's BOM.
- **Estimated vs actual** — both columns filled per build; the gap is the
  lesson.
- Salvage/scavenge counts as $0 in the table with a note of what it was.
