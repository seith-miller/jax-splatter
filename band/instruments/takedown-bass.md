# Takedown bass

**Concept:** Original body design by Jax — a cross between a Steinberger
and a Roland G-707 — headless, with a quick-release takedown neck so the
two halves pack carry-on, and **integrated lighting inside the body**
(house puck architecture: DMX decoder + 12V RGB, PD-brick power).
**Role in the set:** low-end string voice on top of the track — attack and silhouette; the subs already carry the weight.
**Designer/builder:** Jax.
**Stage debut target:** TBD.
**Tier:** $100–500 (hardware; body/neck materials extra if not donor).

## Spec (Jax, 2026-09-09)

- Headless; original body style, Steinberger × Roland G-707.
- Takedown neck joint — packs as two sub-19" pieces (carry-on rule).
- Integrated lighting inside the body, DMX-driven with the house standard.

## Bass-specific design risk

At 34" scale the neck segment alone (~24"+) breaks the carry-on rule.
The design needs **short scale (30" or under)** — or a neck that itself
splits — for the neck half to fit the bag. Verify segment lengths in the
design before cutting anything.
