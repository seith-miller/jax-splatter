# Keytar plate

**Concept:** Buy a battery-powered mini MIDI controller, send MIDI out;
mount it on a **custom backing plate** that gives it a guitar-ish/keytar
feel. This one is **prop design more than instrument making** — the
commercial controller does the work, the plate does the look.
**Role in the set:** keys voice over the track; a strapped, roaming
silhouette instead of a table.
**Stage debut target:** TBD.
**Tier:** $100–500.

## Spec (Jax, 2026-09-09)

- Battery-powered mini controller keyboard, MIDI out.
- Custom backing plate, guitar-ish ergonomics (strap, neck-ish grip zone).
- Prop-grade build; carry-on rule applies (plate splits or fits flat).
