# Rototom chest rack

**Concept:** A small rack of rototoms (classic 6"/8"/10" set) on the same
style of marching chest harness as the kick/snare rig — the ensemble's
roaming tom voice.
**Role in the set:** fills and accents over the track; rototoms are
shell-less and all attack, which is exactly the brief — maximum transient
legibility, minimum weight.
**Stage debut target:** TBD.
**Tier:** $100–500.

## Spec (Jax, 2026-09-09)

- Small rototom rack, chest-mounted on the same kind of harness as the
  kick/snare rig.

## Build notes

- Rototoms have **no shell** — nowhere to hide a light inside. Mount pucks
  on the rack bar under the heads firing upward; rototom heads are single-
  ply and glow well. Design the bracket once, reuse per drum.
