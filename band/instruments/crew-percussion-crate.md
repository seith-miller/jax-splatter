# Crew percussion crate

**Concept:** A crate of grabbable hand percussion — tambourines, wood
blocks, cowbell, claves, shakers, whistles — that arms the whole rotating
cast. Guest vocalists and dancers become musicians for a song. Zero build:
this is a buy list, deployable at the very next gig.
**Role in the set:** distributed transient reinforcement; culturally
pre-loaded visual anchors (everyone knows what a cowbell hit looks like);
the mechanism that makes the pirate-crew ensemble real.
**Stage debut target:** next show.
