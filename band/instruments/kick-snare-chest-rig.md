# Kick/snare chest rig

**Concept:** Marching-percussion chest rig carrying two drums — piccolo
snare left, cut-down 16" floor tom right — the roaming kick/snare of the
ensemble. Drums glow in time with the backing track.
**Role in the set:** transient punctuation over the track's low end (the
subs carry the energy; this carries the hit) plus a lit, legible visual
anchor for the beat.
**Stage debut target:** TBD.
**Tier:** $500+ (phased below).

## Spec (Jax, 2026-09-08)

- **Snare (L):** ~4" piccolo, tuned relatively normally.
- **"Kick" (R):** 16" floor tom, shell depth irrelevant — fine to take a
  16×16 and cut it down substantially. Muffled heads **both sides**
  (cocktail-kit kick style) + foam batting inside to absorb sound.
- **Small gigs:** fully acoustic — no transmission at all.
- **Large venues:** normal drum mics mounted on the rig → two separate
  wireless packs.
- **Light:** one DMX receiver on the rig, daisy-chained to two small par
  lights mounted **inside each drum**, illuminated in time with the
  backing track.

## Build notes

- Translucent muffled heads (Silentstroke-style mesh) glow well from an
  internal par; worth choosing heads partly for light transmission.
- Battery-powered pars with W-DMX built in are an alternative to the
  receiver + daisy-chain if wiring on a moving body proves fragile.
