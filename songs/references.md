# Song references — what we're listening to and why

Pulled from Jax's playlists with `gather`, 2026-09-26. Jax: *"I've gone
through and picked out tracks that already exist. Some of them are just for
inspiration. Some of them are covers and some of them have specific musical
ideas that I want to use in songs."*

Three jobs, and they are not the same job:

| Job | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Inspiration** | the feel to chase — never copied, just aimed at |
| **Cover** | we might actually play it. Mechanical licence owed if a recording reaches a DSP |
| **Steal** | one specific musical idea lifted into an original — a rhythm, a sound, a move |

Which track is which is **not recorded yet** — that's Jax's pass. The lists
below are the raw pull.
