# The stage boxes

**Concept (Jax, 2026-09-09):** Three overhead-bin-sized boxes (22×14×9)
with translucent tops. In transit they carry the ground effects. On stage
they are the set: **drop it, plug in power, plug in DMX, go.** Platform,
lighting feature, and road case in one object — and at 22×14×9, the set
piece is itself carry-on/overhead-bin sized.

## Per-box layout

| Unit | Top | Sides |
|---|---|---|
| Stage right | wash firing straight up through the top | 1–2 washes firing upstage; possibly some downstage |
| Stage left | same | same |
| **Center** | *(projector replaces the upstage-firing lights — fires at the backdrop from inside the box)* | washes as above |

## Structural spec — a performer stands (and dances) on this

- Top: **frosted polycarbonate** (NOT acrylic — shatters), 3/8"–1/2";
  frosting doubles as light diffusion and grip.
- Internal cross-bracing so no unsupported span exceeds ~7"; design load:
  a jumping adult (≥300 lb dynamic).
- Recessed connector panel: power in + DMX in/out (daisy-chain the three).
