# T-bar + pull-down screen — the portable upstage

**Concept (Jax, 2026-09-09):** For drive gigs we can't control the
backline or what's behind the stage — so we bring upstage with us. One
T-bar stand: **two light bars and a laser across the top**, and a
**classroom-style pull-down screen** hanging from the crossbar. Instant
controlled backdrop: projection surface + backlight + laser, venue be
damned.

Drive-kit item (like the fog): rides in the vehicle. Fly shows get
backdrop/stands via rider or rental; the control layer still travels.

## Notes

- **Laser safety is a real thing**: never audience-scan without variance/
  certification; mount high, aim above heads or at the screen; check
  local rules per gig. Budget lasers go in cues, not in eyes.
- Projector (center stage box, floor level) firing up at the screen means
  keystone — test the geometry; the box's projector mount may want a tilt
  wedge.
- Screen doubles as the per-song video surface when the venue wall is bad.
- **A hung fixture needs no outlet.** The beam battery (99.9 Wh, AC outlet,
  airline-legal) runs an 11 W LED par ~6 h at full, ~8 h as a show uses it —
  so a light goes on a beam, a truss or the T-bar's crossbar wherever the
  power isn't. Learned at Interzone XVII (Al's Bar: the I-beam has no
  power). It's in the shared kit list:
  [showkit/checklists/kit.md](https://github.com/smartsquared/showkit/blob/develop/checklists/kit.md).
