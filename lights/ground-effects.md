# Ground effects — the travel policy

Ratified with Jax, 2026-09-09.

| Show type | Policy |
|---|---|
| **Drive shows** | Travel with owned ground FX: compact low-fog machine, fan(s), fluid. |
| **Fly shows** | Heavy iron never flies. Ground FX go on the **tech rider** (promoter sources); local rental is the fallback. |
| **Always** | **Own the control, rent the iron**: the DMX dongle, pucks, cabling, and the show file travel everywhere — any DMX-capable rented/house machine runs *our* cues. |
