Jax Splatter — the color solid, sliced

Four horizontal slices through OKLCh space, one per lightness band. Angle = hue, distance from center = chroma. Each color sits on the slice matching its own lightness, so the ground under every dot is (approximately) that color. Hatched area: colors that don't exist in sRGB at that lightness.

The gamut island grows and shifts as lightness changes: tiny in the darks (why Night and Smog can only huddle at the hub), biggest through the mids where the neons live, and tilted green-yellow in the lights — where Slime sits hard against the boundary: the most vivid green a screen can make. Bone (89°) mirrors Static Gray (267°) exactly across the hub, one slice apart.
