# merch production files — candidates

Print- and cut-ready files for merch round 1, generated by
`tools/merch-files.py` from the signed marks (A002 wordmark via
`brand/reference/wordmark/svg/`, type per A003). Text is outlined; nothing
here depends on an installed font. **Candidates until Jax signs them** —
rows in [../../releases/REGISTRY.md](../../releases/REGISTRY.md).

| Dir | Candidate | Finished size | Sheet | Machine notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `sticker-sheet/` | A007 sticker-sheet | 4 × 1.25 in kiss-cut, r 0.125, 0.06 bleed | 18.5 × 12 in, 32-up | Marksbury Roland, sticker stock. `CutContour` group = magenta stroke, no fill — VersaWorks reads it as the cut layer. Ask for laminate. |
| `button-face/` | A008 button-face | 2.633 in cut for the 2.25 in maker; 2.25 in shows, the rest wraps the rim | 8.5 × 11 in, 12-up | Photo paper; circle cutter at the branch, or the Roland cuts the `CutContour`. Confirm the maker's cut size before printing 5 sheets. |
| `lighter-wrap/` | A011 lighter-wrap | 2.875 × 2.375 in, r 0.06, 0.05 bleed — **test-fit one on a Bic Maxi and adjust the two numbers at the top of `lighter_unit()`** | 18.5 × 12.5 in, 30-up | Same Roland job as the stickers. Wordmark reads bottom-to-top on the front face; URL on the right side face. |
| `cooler-wrap/` | A012 cooler-wrap | 8 × 4 in flat, front panel wordmark, back panel GET SPLATTERED! — **measure the blank** | 8.5 × 14 in, 2-up, **MIRRORED** | Sublimation. The sheet file is mirrored for transfer; the `_unit_preview` is the right-reading view. Cream ground prints as a tint on the white neoprene. |
| `price-card/` | — (table kit, not a release) | 8.5 × 11 in | 1 | Any printer; laminate pouch $0.15. Venmo handle isn't on it — a QR sticker on the day. |

Regenerate:

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install fonttools uharfbuzz qrcode
.venv/bin/python tools/merch-files.py
```
