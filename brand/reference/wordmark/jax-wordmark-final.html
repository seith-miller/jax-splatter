Left: the avatar crop — JAX alone, same spec, for square contexts (social profiles, favicons). Right: the light-ground form — letters switch to Night #05060a, the X stays Splatter Pink. These are the only two sanctioned variations.

Rules. The X is always Splatter Pink — it is the only colored letter, on every ground. Letters are #e8ecf6 on dark grounds, #05060a on light grounds. Always Pathway Gothic One, always one line (or JAX alone). Never stacked, never outlined, never distressed — splatter treatments live around the mark, never in it.
