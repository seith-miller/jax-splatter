# The lore — who Jax Splatter is

Light canon for the character. Dictated by Jax in session 2026-09-17;
his words are quoted where they define something. Like [bio.md](bio.md)
this is ratified in session, not a stamped release. Keep it light — the
audience never gets this document, they get the feeling.

## The logline

Jax Splatter is an interdimensional deadbeat. He skipped out on the
Galactic core owing back taxes, a stack of obligations, and a load of
dirty laundry, and landed on Earth with one plan: put together the
greatest interdimensional show ever staged, on no money, with whoever
shows up. Every ticket, every sticker, every tote bag goes into the show.

## The character (Jax, 2026-09-17)

> "He's kind of a lovable clown."

> "A two-bit grifter, song-and-dance carny who doesn't know what else to
> do."

The mix, in Jax's words: the Flim-Flam Man (George C. Scott, 1967) ×
Professor Harold Hill (*The Music Man*) × Jack Nicholson. Read that as:

| From | Jax gets |
|---|---|
| **The Flim-Flam Man** | the drifting con artist you root for; small towns, small scores, always moving on |
| **Harold Hill** | the pitch — he's selling a town a marching band he can't actually lead, and the town gets a band anyway |
| **Jack Nicholson** | the grin, the eyebrow, the sense that charm and danger are the same thing |

Not a villain, not a hero. A hustler whose hustle is the show.

## What's true

- **Home** is the Galactic core. He owes people there. He is not going back.
- **Earth** is where you go when you can't afford anywhere else.
- **He's a shapeshifter** — "there are times when he can pass as human."
  The mask, harness, cap and cigar are what passing looks like on a good
  night. On a bad night, less so.
- **The splatter is his alien goo.** Splatter Pink is the color of it.
  The splat on the key art, the pink X in the wordmark, the paint on the
  wall after a show — that's him. *Get Splattered* is literal.
- **The show is the plan.** The interdimensional show pays everything off,
  if he can ever mount it. Every gig is a down payment on the big one.
- **The budget is the merch table.** Shoestring by canon: what the table
  makes tonight is what the show can afford tomorrow (D13, the phase map).
- **Mozelle Shante is the ex.** Twice. Bigger than him, always was; the
  divorces are the thing he's best known for back home.
- **The money was cotton candy.** Mogul, past tense. Where it went is
  never explained.
- **Unrepentant alcoholic**, and **incapable of keeping his mouth shut on
  the toilet** — the two ways the press keeps finding him.
- **The crew is whoever he can rope in.** The rotating cast, all wireless,
  all roaming (docs/stage-vision.md) — a pickup band, a pirate crew, a
  marching band that was sold to the town last week.

## The rule: never explain it

> "We don't really explain to the audience. They should kind of feel like
> an alien caravan just drove through their town."

Nothing on the site, nothing in the bio, no origin post. The lore is a
feel, not a text. It leaks out through:

- **the broadcast** — core-side entertainment news covering him as a
  C-list has-been, played between songs (Cowboy Bebop's Big Shot move);
  see [video/interstitials.md](../video/interstitials.md)
- **song titles and lyrics** — the pitch, the come-on, the con
- **merch copy** — the caravan's souvenirs
- **the hotline** — a real number, comedy 1-900 energy (AGENDA item;
  round-2 merch)
- **between-song patter** — Harold Hill working the room
- **the look** — a caravan, not a band: things that fold up and leave

If someone asks, Jax is from out of town.

## Voice

- Filthy, joyful, in on the joke. Never mean, never sad about it.
- Plain words — kid, homie, friend. Never "comrade."
- He is always selling something. He believes it while he's saying it.
- He doesn't know what else to do. That's the whole heart of it.
