# Counts sheet — untitled vogue piece (125.8 BPM)

- **Count**: full-time (1 beat = 1 count); every kick is a beat, every beat is
  counted — per [../README.md](../README.md)
- **One 8-count** = 2 bars = 3.816s; the routine proper is ~15 8s
- **Grid anchor**: count 1.1 = 14.554s in the ref video (first kick of the
  choreography); beat = 0.4770s
- **Track**: unknown — not in the library, Shazam can't place it. **TODO.**
- **Style**: vogue fem
- **Notation**: [../notation.md](../notation.md) — `8.beat` addresses,
  `-` then, `+` together, `(L)`/`(R)` sides
- **Ref video**: `vouge.mp4` in
  [Drive: Jax Splatter/choreo](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1wVs4rjOabhFHXI3Qah7HFvWZIMr7TJU7);
  counted master + step clips in `~/gather/jax-splatter/choreo-vouge/`
- **Steps library**: the Splatter Steps app (Claude artifact) — every bold
  step below has a card with a loop-clean clip

Everything before 0.5 is freeform. The choreographer's call is the count-in:
"5-6-7-8" on 0.5-0.8.

## Structure

count-in -> standing travel (1-2) -> hands block (3) -> MELT (4) ->
floor chapter (5-9, Floor Dip hit at 7.8) -> rise (10) -> duck/strut (11)
-> [uncharted 12+]

## Notes

- 8s 6-9 are deliberately charted as freestyle floor performance: Jax chose
  not to track those movements as named steps (except the Floor Dip). A hired
  dancer works from the floor-performance vocabulary; only 7.8 is fixed.
- The Melt (4) and the rise (10) are the chapter hinges; the rise is untracked
  as a step but its shape is the Melt reversed.
