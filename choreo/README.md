# choreo

Movement and staging: what happens on stage during a set, blocking, guest
performers, and any rehearsed sequences tied to specific tracks.

## Counting convention — locked 2026-08-27

All tracks are 4/4. Counts sheets use dancer 8-counts with a fixed
tempo-to-count mapping, so "hit on 5" always means the same thing:

- **Below 140 BPM**: 1 beat = 1 count. One 8-count = 2 bars.
- **140 BPM and above**: 2 beats = 1 count (half-time). One 8-count = 4 bars.

The 140 boundary counts as half-time. The count-in ("5-6-7-8") is the back
half of the preceding 8 — movement starts on the following 1.
