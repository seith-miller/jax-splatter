# Steps library

The movement vocabulary Jax actually has — named steps, where they come from,
and how many counts they take. Counts sheets reference steps by the **Name**
column, so names here are canonical. Add a row when a step enters the body;
status tracks how gig-ready it is.

Status: `solid` (performable cold) · `working` (drilling) · `learning` (new).

## Notes

- Names are whatever Jax calls them — vernacular names where they exist,
  invented names where they don't. Consistency beats correctness.
- "Counts" is how long one cycle of the step takes at full-time count.
- Ref links: tutorial video, session clip, or counts-sheet where it's used.
