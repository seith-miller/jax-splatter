# The makerspace — Lexington Public Library, both branches

The act's factory for round 1. Free to use with a library card and a signed
Experiential Learning Agreement; only materials cost money. Two branches,
each with a different machine set — round 1 uses both. Facts below were
pulled from lexpublib.org and the posted price lists on 2026-09-10;
anything marked *ask* is unconfirmed and goes on the first-visit checklist.

Commercial use is explicitly welcome — Marksbury's own sample-projects
line reads "print fun stickers for your new business."

## Rules of the room

- Sign the Experiential Learning Agreement once, in person, at any branch.
- Ages 13+ at Eastside; all ages at Marksbury.
- Laser + large-format printer: reserve on weekdays. Everything else:
  walk in during open studio.
- Staff assist during open studio; one-on-one help can be scheduled.
- Groups of 10+: call ahead (a merch-making party with the cast is a
  legitimate use).

## First-visit checklist — questions for staff

**Both**
- Reservation slot length; how far ahead; back-to-back slots allowed?
- Is the $8 credit per branch or per card?
- 3D printer model, PLA colors stocked (need black, neon green, pink),
  who slices (bring 3MF/gcode, or staff-run?), max print hours per job.
- Heat press platen size; is there a cap/hat press anywhere?
- HTV colors stocked (fluorescent pink / neon green?) — otherwise BYO.

**Marksbury**
- Roland model; sticker stock — paper or vinyl? waterproof? laminate
  overlay available? Who sets up the contour cut (CutContour layer)?
- Printable HTV brand — dark-garment compatible?
- Embroidery machine model, hoop sizes, cap frame?, accepted file formats
  (PES / DST), digitizing software on site?
- Small laser: model, metal engraving (fiber/IR?) — the blanks they sell.

**Eastside**
- Big laser model and wattage; **is cast acrylic allowed?** (fluorescent
  acrylic is the keychain plan); max cut thickness; do they stock
  plywood/acrylic/leather or BYO only.
- CNC: model, bed size, materials, staff-run?
- Kiln: what it's for (glass fusing / PMC / ceramics), firing schedule.
- HP 24" banner vinyl — grommets or hemming available?
