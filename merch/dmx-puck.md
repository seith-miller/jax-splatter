# The DMX puck — product concept

Captured 2026-09-08. A hockey-puck-sized, battery-powered, true-DMX RGB
light: DMX decoder board + 12V RGB module + LiPo in a printed enclosure,
~$30 in parts. Born as the internal lighting standard for the act's
instruments (first: the kick/snare chest rig), with a second life as a
micro-manufactured product.

## Why it's interesting

- **Brand-defining build**: making many of these IS the act's aesthetic —
  instruments and rigs that glow in sync, all driven by the same puck.
- **A real market gap**: cheap battery uplights are bulky; photography
  pucks have no DMX; pro battery fixtures (Astera-class) cost hundreds.
  A puck-sized true-DMX light in the tens of dollars has no obvious
  competitor. Jax is not the only person who'd want one.
- **Cross-promotion**: branded pucks in other acts' rigs put the mark on
  other people's stages.

## Power: decided (Jax, 2026-09-09)

No internal battery. The puck takes **12V in**, fed by any **USB-C PD
power bank** through a PD trigger cable (~$8). Consequences: hot-swap
power mid-set, one charger standard across all instruments, no cells in
the puck (cheaper, safer, shippable), parts cost ~$25 before enclosure.
